CHALLENGE:
- Predict sepsis 6 hours ahead.

DATA:
- Data Type: Time-series tabular
- The data is extracted from 2 different hospitals (Hospital A: 20,336 subjects; Hospital B: 20,000 subjects)
- Each row of the table provides a collection of measurements at the same time
- The sepsis labels in the training data have already been shifted ahead by six hours
- There are 40 time-dependent variables
- Entries of NaN (not a number) indicate that there was no recorded measurement of a variable at the time interval.
- Sepsis percentage: 1.3% (extremely imbalanced)
- Missing values percentage: >90% (very high missingness)
- Domain Shift: high variance between sources (hospital A vs hospital B)

Model:
- Model Type: XGBClassifier (it builds decision trees)
- It accepts Nan and doesn't need normalization

APPROACH:
- Step 1: I split the raw data into the 2 distinct hospitals (data/processed/test_hospital_B.parquet & train_hospital_A.parquet)
    * I decided to use hospital A for training and Hospital B for testing.
    * I used parquet instead of csv format because parquet loads the data by columns not by rows and that will help with calculating the medians.

- Step 2: Data truncation
    * In order to prevent data leakage I permanently deleted all rows of data that occured more than 6 hours after Sepsis diagnosis.
    * After 6 hours of positive sepsis label the doctors detected sepsis and sstarting treating it.

- Step 3: Engineering Missingness Flags
    * Before filling any missing data, I created counters for lab orders (Lactate_Order_Count). When a doctor orders a lab test he is getting suspicious and the model should know that.

- Step 4: Cleaning data (filling misssing values)
    * I used forward-fill (ffill) patient-by-patient assuming a patient's vital sign will remain stable until we measure again.
    * I filled any remaining Nan values with hospital A's global medians.
    * To prevent data leakage, we used hospital A's global medians on hospital.

- Step 5: Feature Engineering
    * I used qSOFA and SIRS to build a System_Overload_Score.
    * I made an Age_Frailty_Index.
    * I made rolling windows for mean & std of vitals
    * I made admisssion deltas (changes from hour 1)

- Step 6: Training baseline_model and making error analysis + SHAP analysis to find best features

- Step 8: Hyperparameter tuning

- Step 9: Training final tuned model and saving it

- Step 10: Making inference script (src/inference.py)

- Step 11: Making api script (api/app.py)
